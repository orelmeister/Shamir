## 📝 Lessons Learned

### What Worked ✅

1. **Director signals with high cluster counts** (10+ directors)
   - NESR: 14 directors → +28.3%
   - AMR: 14 directors → +30.9%

2. **Multi-agent LLM consensus**
   - Reduced false positives from single-model bias
   - Both DeepSeek and Gemini must agree for high confidence

3. **Letting winners run**
   - AMR held 28 days through +30% gain
   - No premature exits on profitable positions

4. **Exit Manager discipline**
   - BLND exit caught insider selling signal
   - Realized +4.1% before potential reversal

### What Didn't Work ❌

1. **Officer signals (now weighted 0.5)**
   - Promotional motivation, not conviction
   - Lower historical win rate

2. **Low director count positions**
   - CASS: 10 directors but still -7.1%
   - Single filing clusters less reliable

3. **No politician requirement initially**
   - 14 of 15 positions now flagged for exit
   - Lost opportunity cost on better picks

### Key Insights 💡

1. **Politicians > Directors > Officers** (Signal Quality Hierarchy)
2. **Fresh signals (<14 days) outperform old signals**
3. **Institutional confirmation (13F data) adds validation layer**
4. **Multi-agent debate catches promotional/biased signals**
